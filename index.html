Welcome to SEO Fundamentals

Welcome to this introductory guide on Search Engine Optimization (SEO). SEO is the practice of improving a website to increase its visibility when people search for products or services related to that website on search engines like Google, Bing, and others.

This website is designed for beginners who want to understand the basic concepts, techniques, and terminology of SEO without getting overwhelmed by complex technical details.

Key Terms to Know

  • Search Engine: A software system that is designed to carry out web searches.
  • Organic Traffic: Visitors who arrive at your website as a result of unpaid search results.
  • Ranking: The position at which a website appears in the search engine results pages.

Why SEO Matters Today

In modern web development, creating a beautiful website is only part of the challenge. If search engines can't understand your content, your target audience will never find it. SEO acts as a bridge between the infrastructure of your site and the end user searching for information related to your site.

Benefits include: Increased trust and credibility, increased traffic over time, and a better overall user experience since good SEO typically comes with good design.
