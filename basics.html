What is SEO?

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the process of making improvements to a website so that it appears higher up in search engine results pages (SERPs). The goal is to attract more visitors naturally without paying for advertisements.

Why SEO Matters

SEO matters because the majority of web traffic comes from search engines. If your site isn't optimized, it won't appear on the first page of results, meaning potential visitors will likely never find your website. Good SEO helps build trust with users and provides them with a better overall experience.

Types of SEO

An effective SEO strategy is generally broken down into three main categories:

  • On-Page SEO: This relates to the content on your website. It includes optimizing text, images, and HTML tags (like title tags and meta descriptions).
  • Off-Page SEO: This involves activities outside of your website that affect your rankings. The most common off-page activity is link building (getting other websites to link to yours).
  • Technical SEO: This refers to improving the technical aspects of a website to help search engines crawl and index it more effectively. This includes page speed, mobile compatibility, and site architecture.
